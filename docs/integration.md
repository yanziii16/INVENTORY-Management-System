# Module Integration Agreement Log

## Agreement 1: Library Supplies and Equipment Coordination

- **Modules Involved:** Library and Inventory
- **Agreement Type:** Verbal Agreement
- **Status:** Discussed during the system presentation

### Agreement Details

During the system presentation, the team discussed coordinating with the Library module regarding library supplies and equipment tracked by the Inventory Management Module.

The agreement establishes the need for coordination between the Library and Inventory teams regarding access to stock information and the issuing of items to the Library.

### Items for Confirmation

- The specific inventory information that the Library module may access.
- The method of sharing or retrieving the data.
- The API endpoints and data format, if applicable.
- The responsibilities of each team for maintaining and updating the data.

### Notes

The details listed under Items for Confirmation must be finalized with the Library team before implementation.
